Viagem

A personal flight-comparison desk for one household.

Viagem runs on the household's own computer. It compares flight options, paid in cash, miles or both, for trips the household plans.

When the owner asks it to, Viagem reads the Seats.aero award-alert emails in the owner's own Gmail account, read-only. It uses them to file the award availability those alerts announce beside the owner's saved searches.

Viagem is not offered to the public, and it has no users besides its owner.
